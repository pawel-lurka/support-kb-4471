The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

It covers 374 reports. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. This is the fix that is already queued, and it closes this row only.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY and reads day first

It covers 33 reports. All of them came through the booking form, at the tenants that run booking-v3. The form tells people to type the month first, then reads what they typed the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this form. The hint or the reading must change.

one-day: the date moves by one day

It covers 5 reports. The date is not swapped. It moves by a day: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” That points at time zones, not date order. The desk already noted these reports, then filed them under parser by tenant. The parser fix will not move them back.
